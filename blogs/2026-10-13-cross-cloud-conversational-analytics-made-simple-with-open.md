The reality for most organizations is that data lives everywhere. As engineering teams expand and adopt specialized platforms for their workloads, enterprise data naturally disperses across multiple clouds and operational systems. When analytical and AI initiatives require context from across the entire organization,these teams traditionally face a difficult trade-off: build complex extraction pipelines to copy data into a central repository or accept fragmented reporting.
