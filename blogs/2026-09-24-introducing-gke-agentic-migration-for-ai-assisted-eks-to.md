Enterprises are increasingly standardizing on Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) to run their most critical and AI-driven workloads. From Cloud Storage FUSE for high-throughput data access to custom compute classes (CCC) and advanced GPU slicing, GKE provides the scale and efficiency required for modern applications. However, migrating complex Kubernetes environments from AWS EKS to GKE has traditionally been a daunting, high-friction engineering endeavor.
