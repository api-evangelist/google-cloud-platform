Small teams, short runway, and constant pressure to ship faster — it’s the reality we see every day among the founders we work with at Google for Startups. When you are building a company from the ground up, every hour spent on operational overhead or infrastructure plumbing is an hour taken away from your core product. Last week at Gemini at Work 2026, we introduced the Gemini agent : a single, universal agent designed to work both as your personal assistant and as a dedicated member of your team.
