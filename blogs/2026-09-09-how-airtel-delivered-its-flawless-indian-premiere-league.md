For the millions of fervent fans of the Indian Premiere League (IPL), being able to count on a flawless live streaming cricket experience is never up for debate. For Airtel, producing league TV broadcasts with some of the world's most massive concurrent viewership, dropped packets and buffering are simply not options. During the IPL 2026 season, Airtel partnered with Google Cloud to manage this digital delivery.
