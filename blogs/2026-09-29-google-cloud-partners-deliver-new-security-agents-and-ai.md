As threat actors increasingly use AI to accelerate and develop cyberattacks, enterprise defenders need to rely on both AI and a critical defender’s advantage: Business context that only you possess. Enterprise cyber defense spans identity, network, endpoint, data, cloud, and application layers, often split across a dozen or more products, each with its own context. At Google Cloud Next, we brought partner-built agents into Gemini Enterprise to give you one place to discover and deploy specialized agents across functions including sales, content and creative workflows, HR, and security.
