Imagine your compliance team notices that payments are taking longer than expected to clear. They suspect fraud, and you’re the data scientist helping figure out which transactions to investigate first. Assembling that shortlist requires ingesting and cleaning raw transaction logs, training a model on past fraud, scoring new records, and scheduling the pipeline to run daily.
