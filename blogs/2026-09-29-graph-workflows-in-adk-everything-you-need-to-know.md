Graph engineering is the design work: breaking a task into nodes, connecting them with edges, and deciding where code, models, or people control the next step. The Agent Development Kit (ADK) 's Workflow turns that design into an executable process, with functions and agents doing the work. Through a refund example, this post shows how to run steps in parallel, route decisions, pause for human review, and process a list of cases.
