Enterprises and individual developers frequently run multiple AI inference models. The right architecture can simplify how the models are called while also providing centralized governance. In this post, we'll look at two reference architectures focused on networking AI inference model serving: one for Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) and one all other backend types.
