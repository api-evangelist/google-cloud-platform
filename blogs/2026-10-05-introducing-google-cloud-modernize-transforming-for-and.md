Today, we’re announcing Google Cloud Modernize , an end-to-end transformation portfolio to help enterprises collapse multi-year roadmaps with the power of AI. Google Cloud Modernize brings together Google Cloud’s proven migration and modernization tools, including Migration Center, Google Cloud VMware Engine, Google Cloud Mainframe Modernization and our new EKS-to-GKE Migration Agent , into a single portfolio. With it, enterprise teams now have purpose-built agentic capabilities across infrastructure assessment, platform modernization, and application modernization.
